ChainTrace

Follow every product from factory to shelf.

ChainTrace records each hand-over on Ethereum, so anyone can verify where a product has been.

How it works

  1. ManufacturerRegisters the product and ships it on.
  2. DistributorConfirms arrival, then ships to a retailer.
  3. RetailerReceives the goods and marks them sold.
  4. CustomerLooks up the full journey, no account needed.
